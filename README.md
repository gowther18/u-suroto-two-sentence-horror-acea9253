The Drawing — a 217-character two-sentence horror story. A child's family drawing holds one extra figure, a tall man with no face; the second sentence reveals the man is the father himself, as he looks when he thinks they are asleep. This program writes the story to /output/entry.txt; an AI judge compares the stories two at a time, and the scariest takes the prize.

Run it: `python3 main.py` (writes /output/entry.txt).
